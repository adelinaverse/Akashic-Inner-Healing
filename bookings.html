What it is

A channelled session from your highest self

Think of it as if there were no noise around you at all. A completely quiet room. Just you, and whatever needs to be shown. Not from your mind. From your soul.

It speaks in a gentle whisper, a nudge. It won't hand you every answer, because it's here to empower you to find them yourself. A soft nudge forward, with the keys to unlock the doors in front of you. What you do next is entirely yours.

And it won't hand you sunshine and platitudes either. The Records show you what's actually there, the pattern underneath, the thing you keep circling, and they hold it with love. Sometimes what comes through is tender. Sometimes it's a truth you already half knew. Either way, you don't hold it alone.

"We can only give you the keys. It's up to you whether you open the door."

And what it isn't

Not your future. Not every answer.

This isn't a psychic reading, or a tarot reading. It won't predict your future or tell you every step to take. Your highest self offers only what you need in this moment, because the only way we truly grow is to experience it for ourselves. If every answer were handed to us, what would ever move us to take the step?

One of my clients came carrying something she felt deeply stuck in. Her Records didn't hand her a solution. They gave her homework: to write a prayer for herself. A reminder that she already held the power to move through it.

That's the heart of this work. You leave with keys, not a map drawn by somebody else.

What happens in a reading

How a reading unfolds

There's nothing you need to prepare or perform. Here is how I hold the space, from the first breath to the last.

  1. I ground in

    Before we begin, I take a minute or two to settle, centre, and arrive fully with you.

  2. I ask your permission

    Nothing is ever opened without your consent. Your Records are yours, and we enter only when you say yes.

  3. I read the sacred prayer

    Spoken with your full legal name, this is how I connect into your Akashic field, from the fifth dimension and above.

  4. I speak your questions aloud

    One by one, I ask each question and allow whatever wants to come through to arrive, in its own way and its own time.

What comes can arrive in many forms. Direct channelled words, images, symbols, sometimes a new question in return. Most often I find myself tapped into your own way of sensing, your clair senses: clear feeling, clear knowing, clear hearing, clear smelling, even clear tasting.

I've read two Reiki healers and felt, instantly, exactly how they feel in their own sessions. So a reading often feels deeply familiar, as though it's being spoken in your own intuitive language, even if you've never named it.

You don't need to know any of this to receive it. Come with an open mind and an open heart. That's all.

Before you come

A few soft questions, answered

What are the Akashic Records?

Think of them as a living field that holds the imprint of your soul, everything it has experienced, felt and known across time. The word comes from the Sanskrit "akasha," the space that holds all things. It isn't a place you visit so much as a frequency you can attune to, where your soul's story is held in love. A reading is simply me tuning in and listening on your behalf.

Where is the information coming from?

From your own highest self, and from the guides, teachers and loved ones who hold your Records with care. I connect in through a sacred prayer, from the fifth dimension and above, and I speak back what comes through. Nothing is invented and nothing is forced. I'm only the channel. The wisdom is already yours.

Do I need to know anything beforehand?

No. Just your questions and an open heart. Everything else, I hold.

How do I prepare for the reading?

Very little is needed. Find a quiet spot where you won't be interrupted, and come with an open mind and an open heart. If you have questions, sit with them beforehand and notice which ones tug at you. If they haven't formed yet, that's okay too. The guide I send you is there to help, and you can send your questions and photo to me on WhatsApp whenever you're ready.

Why do you need my full legal name?

Your reading opens through a sacred prayer, spoken with your full legal name, the name your soul is recorded under. It's simply how the connection is made.

What about a photo?

A photo helps me connect with your energy before we begin. You can add one when you book, or simply send it to me on WhatsApp beforehand, whichever feels easier for you.

Do I need to know my clair senses?

Not at all. I meet you wherever you naturally sense, and many people discover theirs in the reading itself.

How can I integrate what comes through?

Let it land gently rather than trying to hold all of it at once. Writing it down helps, and so does noticing what stirred something in your body as you heard it. Then choose one small thing to act on, and let the rest unfold in its own time. The Records give you the keys, and integration is you choosing, softly, which doors to open.

Is this religious?

No. The Records draw on something older and wider than any single tradition. You're welcome here exactly as you are, whatever you believe.
