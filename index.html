Frequently Asked Questions

Can the Akashic Records really be learned online?

Yes. The Akashic Records are not limited by physical distance, making them particularly well-suited to online learning. Students around the world successfully learn to access and work within the Akashic field through online training, guided practice, and remote attunements.

This course combines comprehensive teachings, live Zoom sessions, practical exercises, and a distant attunement to help you develop a strong and confident connection to the Records. Just as the Akashic Records can be accessed regardless of location, the attunement and energetic transmissions can also be received remotely while maintaining their effectiveness and integrity.

Many students find that learning online offers the added benefit of being able to practice in the comfort of their own space and revisit the recordings as they deepen their experience with the Records.

Will I receive enough support throughout the training?

Absolutely. In addition to the comprehensive pre-recorded lessons, you will have access to live Zoom sessions where we can practice together, deepen your understanding and answer any questions that arise. You will also be invited to join a dedicated WhatsApp community where you can connect with fellow students, share experiences, ask questions and receive ongoing support throughout the course.

What if I can't attend the live Zoom calls?

No problem. We understand that students join from different countries and time zones. If you are unable to attend a live session, you can submit your questions via email or through the WhatsApp group. The recordings of the live calls will be uploaded to your training portal so you can watch them at your convenience.

How long will I have access to the course materials?

You will have access to the course portal, training materials and all recordings for six months following the final live Zoom session. This gives you ample time to revisit the lessons, deepen your practice and integrate the teachings at your own pace.

Will I receive a certificate upon completion?

Yes. Upon completing the training requirements, you will receive an internationally accredited Akashic Records certification accredited by the International Practitioners of Holistic Medicine (IPHM).

What is the cancellation policy?

Due to the digital nature of this training all enrollments are non-refundable. Please ensure that you are ready to commit to the training before enrolling. If you have any questions prior to registration, we encourage you to reach out and we will be happy to help you determine whether the course is the right fit for you.
